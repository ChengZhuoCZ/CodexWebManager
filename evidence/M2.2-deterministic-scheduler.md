## Acceptance mapping

### Deterministic selection

- The scheduler is synchronous and pure with respect to candidates plus one injected clock read.
- Permutations of equal candidates always select the same bounded ASCII account ID.
- Frozen decision/evaluation objects provide a stable, explainable ranking.
- Explicit exclusion lists are validated, deduplicated, and cannot silently name an unknown
  account.

### Priority, quota, cooldown, and concurrency

- Candidate metadata is limited to the M1.2 public account shape plus runtime count, cooldown, and
  the M2.1 quota snapshot.
- Fresh complete quota uses `min(five_hour, weekly)`; a zero in either window is exhausted.
- Within the same certainty tier, higher numeric priority is preferred before quota, confidence,
  lower concurrency utilization, and the final account-ID tie-break.
- Active future cooldowns and accounts at `max_concurrency` are ineligible with explicit reasons;
  the exact cooldown boundary becomes eligible.
- Disabled and caller-excluded accounts are also explicit rather than silently dropped.

### Exhausted and stale-data cases

- When every fresh complete snapshot has a zero window, the result is
  `all_accounts_unavailable` with no selected account and per-account `quota_exhausted` reasons.
- Scheduler time re-evaluates staleness from `observed_at` and the snapshot threshold, preventing a
  once-fresh object from remaining fresh indefinitely.
- Stale, partial, and unavailable snapshots never contribute cached numeric quota to ordering.
  They remain eligible only as an explicit uncertain fallback with `remaining_ratio: null`.
- This policy avoids interpreting unknown data as either 0 or 1 while allowing a later bounded
  upstream attempt to determine actual availability.

## Residual risks

- Error-specific cooldown policy, half-open probing, and persistence remain M2.3.
- Routing-session ownership and within-stream invariants remain M2.4.
- Selecting an uncertain fallback does not prove it has quota; the M3 proxy must classify the
  bounded upstream result and must not retry after semantic output.
- Real account and cross-account behavior remain deferred and unverified.
