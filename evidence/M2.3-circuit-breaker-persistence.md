## Acceptance mapping

### Distinct cooldowns

- `quota_exhausted`, `auth_expired`, `rate_limited`, `network_error`, and `upstream_5xx` each require
  an explicit independently configured duration.
- Test policy uses five distinct durations and verifies the exact `cooldown_until` for every kind.
- `Retry-After` is accepted only for `rate_limited`, cannot shorten its base policy, and is clamped
  to a hard configured maximum.
- Invalid failure kinds, policies, retry values, clocks, and timestamps fail closed.

### Bounded half-open probing

- Open circuits deny acquisition before the cooldown boundary and transition at the exact boundary.
- Half-open acquisition issues explicit runtime-only probe leases up to `halfOpenMaxProbes` (1–16).
- A successful lease closes the circuit and resets consecutive failures.
- A failed lease reopens the circuit with its classified cooldown and invalidates every outstanding
  lease; canceled probes can be released and replaced within the bound.
- Stale, duplicate, missing, or clock-rollback probe completions cannot bypass the bound.

### State persists across restart

- `exportState()` emits deterministic version-1 documents sorted by internal account ID.
- The state store writes a mode-0600 temporary file, synchronizes it, atomically renames it, and
  synchronizes the private directory where supported.
- Loads are size-bounded before and during reading, use no-follow semantics, verify the opened inode,
  and validate every document field before returning it.
- A simulated restart saves an open circuit, constructs a new breaker from disk, and compares the
  restored state with the original.
- Half-open state is persisted, but runtime lease tokens and in-flight counts are not; restore begins
  at zero in-flight probes.
- Tests reject permissive directories/files, symlinks, corrupt/empty/oversized input, unsafe file
  names, and invalid saves while preserving the last valid file.
